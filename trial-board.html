How this works

Book a trial by clicking a slot in the grid — it saves straight away and everyone who opens this page sees it within a few seconds, no save button needed. The grid shows the current week; use Prev/Next to flip weeks. Once a trial's date has passed, it drops into "Needs follow-up" until someone marks it Signed up or No show. About an hour before each trial (5pm the day before for 5am classes), a reminder email goes to the studio inbox with who's coming, for what class, and with which coach. The "Copy text reminders" tab gives you a ready-made text for every upcoming trial — tap Copy text and paste it into Messages to remind the trialist yourself.
